description
Survive a city that reacts to every gunshot. Lure zombie hordes with noise devices, then break through with fire, lightning and weapon upgrades.

Explore 12 sequential missions and face a mid-boss and final boss in each. Learn the patterns of 24 bosses in the codex. Combine a shotgun or electric nailgun with 12 weapon upgrades, then reinforce armor and magazines using recovered parts.

Move with touch controls and switch between automatic and manual firing. Leaving the app pauses the operation; continue from a locally saved run.

Game interface language: Korean. This version includes no login, advertisements or real-money purchases. Game code and artwork are bundled in the app. Progress is stored on the device; cloud sync and automatic transfer of browser saves are not provided.
